# Happy Birthday Amma ❤️ – A Surprise From Your Pandu

A premium, interactive multi-screen birthday surprise website built with love for Amma.

## ✨ Features & Screen Flow

1. **Screen 1 – Surprise Envelope**:
   - Animated pastel envelope with heart wax seal
   - Gentle floating hearts, sparkles, and greeting: *"Hey Amma! 💗 Someone special has a little surprise for you..."*
   - Interactive opening animation revealing the letter before transitioning to the surprise hub.

2. **Screen 2 – Four Surprise Gifts (2×2 Grid)**:
   - **Card 1 (🎂 Birthday Cake)**: Leads to Make a Wish & celebration.
   - **Card 2 (📸 Our Memories)**: Scrapbook photo gallery with washi tape & polaroids.
   - **Card 3 (💌 A Letter for Amma)**: Heartfelt handwritten letter from Pandu with delicate flowers & wax seal.
   - **Card 4 (🎁 Special Surprise)**: Cake sharing animation and grand finale hug!
   - Tracks visited status so Amma knows which surprises she has opened.

3. **Screen 3 – Make a Wish**:
   - Two-tier pink & cream birthday cake with 3 glowing candles, frosting & hearts.
   - *"Blow the Candles 🕯️"* button with realistic candle blowout, smoke wisps, and celebratory confetti.
   - *"May all your wishes come true, Amma! ❤️"* message.

4. **Screen 4 – Birthday Celebration**:
   - *"HAPPY BIRTHDAY MY DEAREST AMMA ❤️"* with floating pastel balloons, glowing lights, and a message of gratitude.

5. **Screen 5 – Our Memories (Scrapbook Gallery)**:
   - Polaroid-style frames with washi tape and captions.
   - **Easy Customization**: Tap the upload icon on any card to replace placeholder illustrations with real family photos of Amma and family! Photos persist in your browser's localStorage.
   - Edit captions and titles directly in the browser.

6. **Screen 6 – A Letter From Your Pandu**:
   - Cream paper background with floral decorations and ruled paper styling.
   - Exact emotional letter with gentle typewriter reveal and instant-read options.

7. **Screen 7 – Let's Eat Cake**:
   - Adorable cartoon characters (Amma & Pandu) sharing cake.
   - Interactive cake feeding with cute bite crumbs!
   - *"I Am Full! 😋"* button revealing: *"But there's always room for Amma's love! ❤️"*.

8. **Screen 8 – Final Surprise & Hug**:
   - Grand celebration with the World's Best Mother Award.
   - *"Send Amma a Hug 🤗"* button triggering a full-screen animated warm hug modal:
     *"I LOVE YOU SO MUCH AMMA! ❤️ Forever your little child. 💗"*

9. **Music & Audio Engine**:
   - Zero-dependency Web Audio API music-box playing a soothing lullaby arrangement of *"Happy Birthday"*.
   - Never autoplays (toggleable with the floating music button).
   - Gentle SFX for blowing candles, chimes, pops, and warm hugs.

## 🚀 Running the Project

```bash
# Install dependencies (already installed)
npm install

# Start development server
npm run dev
```

Visit [http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/) in your browser.
